Our Story

Shri Swaminarayan Gurukul Public School is a co-educational institution affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), situated in Ankleshwar, Bharuch district, Gujarat, India.

We combine the timeless values of the Gurukul system with modern pedagogy and infrastructure. Our school is built on the belief that every child deserves a nurturing environment where they can grow intellectually, socially, physically, and morally. Located in the heart of GIDC, Ankleshwar, we serve families who seek quality education rooted in Indian values and aligned with national standards.

Management

Shree Swaminarayan Gurukul Public School is managed by Shree Swaminarayan Sevaniketan. The trust is committed to providing accessible, value-based education and to running the institution with transparency, discipline, and a focus on the overall development of every student.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop young and responsible citizens with active and creative minds, compassion for others and courage to have the right beliefs. We stress on overall development – intellectual, social, physical, moral and spiritual.

Our Vision

Gurukul Public School aims at being an excellent social institution that contributes to the rapidly changing society and thus making the world a better and more just place.
