About Us

Welcome to Shri Swaminarayan Gurukul Public School — a place where tradition meets modern education.

We are a co-educational school affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), located in Ankleshwar, Bharuch district, Gujarat, India. Our mission is to nurture young minds with values, discipline, and excellence in academics.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop young and responsible citizens with active and creative minds, compassion for others and courage to have the right beliefs. We stress on overall development – intellectual, social, physical, moral and spiritual.

Our Vision

Gurukul Public School aims at being an excellent social institution that contributes to the rapidly changing society and thus making the world a better and more just place.

Why Choose Us

  • Quality Education

    CBSE curriculum with a focus on holistic development and academic excellence.

  • Values & Discipline

    Rooted in the Gurukul tradition with emphasis on character and values.

  • Safe Environment

    A supportive, inclusive environment for every student to thrive.
